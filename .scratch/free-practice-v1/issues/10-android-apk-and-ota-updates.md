# 10: Android APK + over-the-air updates

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Blocked by:** 01 (Tracer: answer a sample Question on your phone)

## What to build

Your friend installs the app on Android as a regular APK. A published update (new Exams, fixes) reaches both the APK and the iPhone (Expo Go) without reinstalling.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] EAS Build produces an APK that installs and launches on its own on an Android phone.
- [ ] An EAS Update channel is set up. A pushed change shows up on the Android APK and in Expo Go on the iPhone without reinstalling.
- [ ] The README explains how to publish an update.
- [ ] No paid Apple Developer account is used.

Human step: the project owner signs in to their Expo account.
