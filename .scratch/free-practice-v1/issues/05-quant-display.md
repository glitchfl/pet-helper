# 05: Quant display: math, Figures, image Options, Formula Sheet

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Blocked by:** 01 (Tracer: answer a sample Question on your phone)

## What to build

Quantitative Questions read like the exam:
- fractions, roots, exponents and π are properly typeset inside Hebrew right-to-left text
- geometry Figures appear with their Question
- Options that are pictures can be tapped like text Options
- a Formula Sheet button opens NITE's formula page during Quantitative practice

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Rich text supports inline LaTeX math, rendered correctly inside right-to-left Hebrew text.
- [ ] A Question's Figure is shown with it.
- [ ] Image Options can be tapped and get the same feedback as text Options.
- [ ] Figures and image Options sit on a light card, so they stay legible on a dark background.
- [ ] A Formula Sheet button appears only in Quantitative practice and opens the Formula Sheet image.
- [ ] The sample bank gains Quantitative Questions with math, a Figure and image Options, plus a sample Formula Sheet.
- [ ] Checked by hand on the iPhone: fractions, roots, exponents and π look right.
